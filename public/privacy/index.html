Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Lulla is a calm, local-first parent and family tracker. This page explains what Lulla stores, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account for family sync): your email address and display name.
  • Child profile details: your child's first name, birth date and sex, used to label the app and tailor what it shows.
  • Activity entries you log, such as feeds, sleep, diapers, routines and milestones, with any notes you type.
  • Photos you attach to milestones stay on your device only. They are never uploaded.

Where your data lives

By default everything is stored on your own device, in your browser or app storage. If you choose to turn on family sync, your account details, child profile details and activity entries are also stored in a Neon Postgres database so that the people in your family household can see the same logs on their devices. Sync is opt in; if you never create an account, nothing leaves your device.

No ads, no analytics, no trackers

Lulla shows no advertising, runs no analytics, and includes no third-party tracking or advertising code. We do not sell or share your data.

Exporting your data

You can export your data at any time from Settings > Your data: a CSV export per child, and a full JSON backup that you can restore later.

Deleting your data

You can delete your family data from inside the app (Settings > Account > Delete my data), which removes it from the sync server and the device. Your sign-in account remains until removed on request: we will remove your sign-in on request, by emailing support@lulla.dev. See our data deletion page. You can also erase everything stored on a device from Settings > Your data.

Retention

We keep your synced data until you delete your family data or the family household. Data kept only on your device stays there until you erase it or remove the app.

Children's information

Lulla is meant to be used by parents and caregivers, not by children. The child details you enter are used only to run the app for your family.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: support@lulla.dev.
