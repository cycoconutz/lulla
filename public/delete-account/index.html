Delete your Lulla account

Applies to the Lulla app and website at lulla.dev

Option 1: delete your account in the app

  1. Open Lulla and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and find the Account card at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your sign-in and any family you own from our servers, and erases Lulla data on that device. If the app reports that your account was not fully deleted, try again or email us.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you cannot use the app, email support@lulla.dev from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in details: email address, display name, password record and sessions.
  • Any family you own, including its child profiles, activity entries and invite codes. Its other members lose access to the shared data on our server.
  • Your membership in any family you joined.

What can remain

  • Entries you added to a family owned by someone else stay with that family, as part of its shared history.
  • Data already saved on other devices, yours or a family member's, stays there until erased in Settings > Your data or the app is removed.
  • If you turned on lock-screen reminders on other devices, turn them off there. Deleting your account only tries to unregister the device you delete it from, which can fail if the reminder service cannot be reached.
  • Copies may persist for a limited time in our database provider's backup and restore history.

Photos are never uploaded, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Timing

In-app deletion takes effect on our servers right away, apart from the items listed above.

You can export your data first from Settings > Your data. See also our privacy policy.
