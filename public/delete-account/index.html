Delete your Lulla data

Applies to the Lulla app and website at lulla.dev

Option 1: delete your data in the app

  1. Open Lulla and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and find the Account card at the top.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This removes your household data from our sync server (including any family household you own) and erases Lulla data stored on that device. Your sign-in account (email address and display name) remains until you ask us to remove it: we will remove your sign-in on request, by emailing support@lulla.dev.

Option 2: ask us by email

Email support@lulla.dev from the email address on the account and ask us to remove your sign-in and any remaining data. We will confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in details (email address and display name): removed when you request it by email.
  • Your family sync data: child profile details and activity entries stored on our server, including any household you own.
  • Your membership in any household you joined.

Photos are never uploaded, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Data stored on your own devices is removed by the in-app deletion, or by erasing it in Settings > Your data or removing the app.

Timing

In-app deletion of your household data takes effect immediately on the sync server. To have your sign-in removed, email support@lulla.dev.

You can export your data first from Settings > Your data. See also our privacy policy.
